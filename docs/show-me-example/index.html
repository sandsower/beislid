01

Why this exists

Frame the situation where show-me is better than chat or terminal output.

Don't tell me. Show me.

A normal agent answer is fine for short claims. show-me is for work that needs a durable artifact someone can inspect later.

  • Verification: prove commands passed and preserve the logs.
  • Review: explain a diff in a browsable way.
  • Understanding: turn a subsystem or product decision into cards, comparisons, and decision points.
  • Demos: collect screenshots, videos, GIFs, and command evidence in one place.
02

What the deck captures

Show the reusable evidence model.

01

Command logs

What it proves

Exact command, cwd, exit code, timestamps, redacted stdout/stderr

Why it helps

A reviewer can audit the claim without rerunning everything

02

Media assets

What it proves

Copied screenshots/videos/GIFs/SVGs with hashes and captions

Why it helps

UI behavior or visual state is inspectable, not just described

03

NEEDS_CAPTURE blocks

What it proves

What evidence is missing and why

Why it helps

The deck is honest when tooling or context is unavailable

04

Provenance

What it proves

Repo, branch, commit, dirty state, asset/log metadata

Why it helps

The deck remains understandable after the chat scrolls away

05

When to use it

Give concrete trigger examples.

Good prompts

  • Make a proof deck for this PR.
  • Show me this feature works.
  • Make a visual walkthrough of this diff.
  • Document this subsystem as an HTML deck.
  • Turn this product decision into comparison cards.

Bad fit

  • Tiny one-line answer.
  • Work that should stay in chat.
  • Anything that would imply proof without fresh evidence.
